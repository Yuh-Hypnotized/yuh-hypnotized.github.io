Chunk Devastator 26.2

A brutal-survival Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.2: take any damage and the chunk you stand on gets a 5-second countdown — then the whole 16×16 column is deleted into the void. Only end portal frames survive. Multiplayer-ready with warnings visible to all players, parallel countdowns per chunk, and doomed-chunk state persisted across deaths, logouts, and server restarts.
