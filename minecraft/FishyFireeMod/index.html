FishyFiree Mod 1.8.9

A Minecraft Forge client-side HUD mod for 1.8.9 featuring a real-time inventory Counter HUD (tracks blocks & arrows with color-coded thresholds) and a Player Tracker HUD (shows nearest player per team color with distance, height delta, and directional arrows). Both HUDs support draggable positioning, toggle keybinds, and in-game settings GUIs with persistent config.
