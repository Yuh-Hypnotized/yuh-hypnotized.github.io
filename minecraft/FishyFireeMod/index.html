FishyFiree Mod 1.8.9

A Forge mod for Minecraft 1.8.9 featuring an inventory Counter HUD (tracks blocks & arrows with color-coded counts and threshold alerts) and a PlayerTracker compass HUD (shows nearest player per team color with distance and Y-delta). Both HUDs support draggable positioning, toggle hotkeys, and in-game settings GUIs.
