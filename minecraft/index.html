FishyFiree Mod

1.8.9

A Forge mod for Minecraft 1.8.9 featuring an inventory Counter HUD that tracks blocks and arrows with color-coded counts, and a PlayerTracker compass that shows the nearest player in each team color along with distance and Y-delta. Both HUDs are fully customizable with draggable positioning, toggle hotkeys, and in-game settings GUIs.
