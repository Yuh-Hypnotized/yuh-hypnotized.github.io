Chunk Devastator

26.2

A brutal-survival Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.2: take any damage and the chunk you stand on gets a 5-second countdown — then the whole 16×16 column is deleted into the void, bedrock and chest contents included. Only end portal frames survive. Multiplayer-ready with warnings visible to all players, parallel countdowns per chunk, and doom state persisted across deaths, logouts, and server restarts.

FishyFiree Mod

1.8.9

A Forge mod for Minecraft 1.8.9 featuring an inventory Counter HUD that tracks blocks and arrows with color-coded counts, and a PlayerTracker compass that shows the nearest player in each team color along with distance and Y-delta. Both HUDs are fully customizable with draggable positioning, toggle hotkeys, and in-game settings GUIs.
